# Privacy Policy for StreakBuild

**Effective Date:** August 2, 2026

Welcome to **StreakBuild**. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

---

# 1. Information We Collect

When you use StreakBuild, we may collect the following information:

## Personal Information

- Name
- Email Address
- Gender
- Age
- Height
- Weight
- Target Weight
- Fitness Goal
- Activity Level
- Workout Experience
- Workout Preferences

## Fitness Data

The app stores information related to:

- Workout progress
- Completed workouts
- Calories burned
- Water intake
- Daily streaks
- XP and achievements
- Diet preferences

---

# 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account
- Personalize workout plans
- Track your workout progress
- Track your fitness streak
- Calculate calories and statistics
- Provide diet recommendations
- Send workout and water reminders
- Improve app performance and user experience

---

# 3. Firebase Authentication

StreakBuild uses **Firebase Authentication** to securely manage user accounts.

Firebase may collect information necessary for authentication such as:

- Email address
- User ID

For more information:

https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

---

# 4. Cloud Firestore

We use **Google Cloud Firestore** to securely store your account information and workout-related data.

Your information is protected using Firebase Security Rules so users can only access their own data.

---

# 5. Google AdMob

StreakBuild displays advertisements using **Google AdMob**.

AdMob may collect information including:

- Advertising ID
- Device information
- IP address
- App interactions

This information is used to display relevant advertisements and improve ad performance.

Learn more:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

---

# 6. Notifications

The app may send notifications for:

- Workout reminders
- Water reminders
- Daily fitness reminders

You can disable notifications anytime from your device settings.

---

# 7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Your data is stored securely using Firebase services and encrypted communication (HTTPS).

However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

---

# 8. Data Sharing

We do **not sell** your personal information.

We only share information with trusted service providers required to operate the app, including:

- Google Firebase
- Google AdMob

---

# 9. Children's Privacy

StreakBuild is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will remove the information.

---

# 10. Your Rights

You may:

- Update your profile information
- Reset your password
- Delete your account (if supported)
- Contact us regarding your personal information
