Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Open the login screen and tap "Forgot Password". Follow the instructions sent to your registered email address.

How do I change my workout plan?

Update your fitness profile and goals from the Profile section. StreakBuild will generate a new personalized workout plan based on your updated information.

How do I report a bug?

Please send us an email including:

  • Your device model
  • Android version
  • App version
  • Description of the issue
  • Screenshots (if available)

How do I request a new feature?

We welcome suggestions for improving StreakBuild. Send your ideas by email and we'll consider them for future updates.

Response Time

We aim to respond to support requests within 2–3 business days.
